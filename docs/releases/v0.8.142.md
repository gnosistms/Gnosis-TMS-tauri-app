# Gnosis TMS 0.8.142

## Book export

- The EPUB title page sets the title and the subtitle each on one line and
  centres them; the reader wraps them to fit the screen. Line breaks typed into
  the title page's Title field still lay out the PDF and Vellum title pages.
- The publisher logo on the EPUB title page is about the size it is in the PDF,
  instead of filling the width of the page.
- Images downloaded for an export are kept for 7 days, so exporting a book as
  EPUB and then as PDF or DOCX downloads its images only once. Each format still
  prepares the images its own way.

## Glossary Research

- Each step has a sentence under its title saying what it does. Assign meanings
  names its sample and corpus.
- When the AI proposes a step 6 translation that fits none of the meanings, the
  step no longer stops with an error. It suggests the meanings those
  translations would fit, each one editable, with up to 15 paragraphs that use
  it from the sample and then the rest of the corpus. "Add selected meanings"
  adds them and reruns 4.6 Assign meanings and the steps after it. "Continue
  without adding" finishes step 6 without those translations.
- Meaning definitions state the meaning itself, as a dictionary does, without
  phrases about who uses the word or why.
- Re-researching an existing entry from step 2b reruns step 2a only when the
  entry adds a source form that 2a has not listed. Otherwise research continues
  past 2b and keeps your selection of forms.
- Step 6 no longer shows the guidance paragraphs on selecting translations and
  keeping usage notes brief.
- Checkboxes are always in the accent colour. On macOS, some turned gray after
  the window had been inactive.
