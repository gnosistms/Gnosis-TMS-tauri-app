# Gnosis TMS 0.8.112

## Clearer editor selection

- The row connected to the right panel now has an outline, without changing its background color.
- When the connected row scrolls out of view, an arrow and message above the sidebar tabs let you bring it back into view.
- Filtering out the row disconnects the panel. Deleted rows can stay connected while their section is expanded; closing the section or permanently deleting the row disconnects it.

## AI Assistant scrolling

- Typing in a full-height AI Assistant text box no longer makes the conversation scrollbar jump.
- Resizing the text box preserves your position while reading older messages and follows the latest reply only if you were already at the bottom.
- Text wrapping at fractional widths no longer clips the last line of the text box.
