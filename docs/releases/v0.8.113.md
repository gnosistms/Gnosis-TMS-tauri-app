# Gnosis TMS 0.8.113

## More direct editor selection

- Click the empty space in a translation card to select its row and language without opening the text for editing. The whole card is selectable, including its outer edges and the space beside a language divider.
- The selected language now has a small orange label, while the selected row keeps its orange outline. Both stay visible while you use the panel on the right.
- If a row needs to refresh before it can be selected, a later selection now takes priority. The older row will not take focus or reopen for editing after its refresh finishes.

## Clearer panel states

- AI Assistant, Review, History, and Comments remain available when no translation is selected. Each tab shows a compact message until you select a translation.
- The AI Assistant no longer displays a disabled prompt box when there is no selected translation.
