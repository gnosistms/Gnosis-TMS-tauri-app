# Gnosis TMS 0.8.126

## Glossary Research

- A new **Research** button in the glossary editor researches how a term
  should be translated. The app reads the paragraphs where the term is used
  in the team's texts, looks the term up in a dictionary and in the source
  books the author drew on, and proposes a glossary entry: the term's
  meanings, a translation for each, and when to use which.
- Before anything is saved you review the proposal: remove or add
  translations, then either let an AI try them on sample paragraphs and count
  which translation it chooses for each use, or open the report straight
  away. The report shows the entries per meaning with examples, and Save
  writes them into the glossary.
- When a typed term is never found in the texts, the app suggests the
  spellings the texts actually use, so you can pick the right one.
- While research runs, a numbered list shows its steps. You can pause it,
  resume an interrupted run, or skip optional steps. Past research on a
  glossary is listed, with a ✓ on the studies already saved into the
  glossary.
- New research uses the quicker **Focused research**. The longer **Full
  diagnostic research** (second AI judge, general-language frequency checks,
  a consistency test) is available under Advanced.

## Fixes

- Typing in search boxes with an input method such as the Windows Vietnamese
  Telex keyboard no longer scrambles the text (#357).
- Project search lists projects and chapters in natural name order, and the
  search is kept when you open a result in the editor and come back (#352).
- The AI Assistant no longer puts a sent message back into the message box
  (#355).
- Progress and waiting windows on Windows no longer show a dark focus ring
  around the window (#356).
