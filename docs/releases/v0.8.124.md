# Gnosis TMS 0.8.124

## Marking a row counts as checking it

- **Changed after my last edit** now shows rows another person edited after you
  last checked them. Marking a row reviewed, flagging it "Please check" or
  removing your flag counts as checking it, just like editing it. A reviewed
  mark set by an AI Review you started counts as yours.
- A mark in any language counts for the whole row.
- Un-reviewing a row yourself, one row at a time or with "Mark all unreviewed",
  undoes your earlier reviewed mark in that language.
- Other people's marks and comments never make a row show; only their edits do.
- The changes shown are the ones made since you last checked the row.
