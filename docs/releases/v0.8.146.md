# Gnosis TMS 0.8.146

## Audio and video

- PDF, DOCX, RTF, Markdown, TXT, Vellum, plain-text copy and the book preview
  now put a row's audio in a footnote on the row's text, instead of an
  "Audio:" line in the middle of the page. The note gives the caption, then
  the audio's address, linked where the format has links. EPUB does the same
  for audio it cannot include. HTML and WordPress exports keep the player.
- Because the audio footnote needs text to attach to, audio can no longer be
  added to a row with no text in that language. Inserting, uploading, dropping
  or duplicating audio into an empty language says why, and nothing is saved.
- Messages, dialogs, tooltips and labels about a row's media say "audio file"
  or "video" when that is what the row holds, instead of "image".
- The "Changed after my last edit" view shows a previous audio or video value
  as its tile, crossed out, instead of a broken image.
- Uploading a video file, audio other than MP3, M4A or WAV, or a file over
  25 MB now says why the file was refused and what to do instead: insert the
  video by URL, convert the audio, or make the file smaller.

## Glossary

- When the tooltip on a source term shows exactly one translation, it says
  "Command + C to copy" (Ctrl on Windows and Linux), and that shortcut copies
  the translation.

## Exports

- With "Show links in footnotes as plain text" on, a link in an image caption
  is now followed by its address in brackets, as footnote links are, in PDF,
  DOCX, RTF, plain-text copy and Vellum.

## AI review

- Full review no longer flags explanatory translations. When a translator
  paraphrases or adds clarifying words because a literal translation would be
  hard for readers to understand, the review accepts it as long as it says
  what the author meant. It suggests a correction only where the translation
  misunderstands the source.
