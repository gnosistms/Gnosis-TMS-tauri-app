# Gnosis TMS 0.8.157

## New AI model notices for team Owners

- Gnosis TMS checks once a week in the background for new models from AI providers selected by teams you own. The first check records the models already available. When a later check finds new ones, you’ll see their names the next time you’re working as Owner in any of those teams.
- Choose **Ignore** to dismiss the notice or **AI Settings** to open the active team’s settings. Each notice appears once per account on this device, even when several teams use the same provider.
- Opening the **Model** dropdown in AI Settings marks the models it shows as seen. If you just visit AI Settings, you can still receive a notice about those models.
