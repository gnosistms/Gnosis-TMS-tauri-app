# Gnosis TMS 0.8.150

## Editor

- The sidebar tabs sit directly below the **Show row** banner. The conversation fills the remaining space, and the message box stays at the bottom.
- The H1 button’s tooltip now reads **Chapter title**.

## Exports

- Use H1 for chapter titles, H2 for sections, and H3 for subsections. H2 and H3 now use the section and subsection styles from before H3 was introduced. The editor, book preview, and formatted exports reflect that hierarchy.
- The book’s **Include section headings (H2)** option adds sections to the table of contents. EPUB navigation groups subsections under their sections, and Vellum uses its first and second subhead levels.
- Removed the heading-level explanation from the single-chapter WordPress export panel.
