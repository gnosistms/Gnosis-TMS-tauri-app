# Gnosis TMS 0.8.125

## More natural AI translations of spoken language

- AI Translate now translates people's speech the way someone would say it in
  the target language. Words a speaker uses to keep a conversation going, such
  as "right?", "you know", "um", "o sea", "euh" or 就是, are rendered the way a
  native speaker would say them, or left out, instead of word for word. A
  speaker who ends a sentence with "right?" no longer sounds as if she were
  asking the audience a question. Real questions stay questions, and literary
  passages stay literary.
- Subtitle files imported from SRT are translated in stretches of up to 30
  lines, and the AI may move a few words between neighbouring lines so that a
  sentence running across several lines reads naturally. Every line still gets
  its own translation, and speaker markers (>>) and sound tags such as [music]
  stay on their lines.
- AI Review (translation accuracy) reads neighbouring subtitle lines together,
  so words moved from one line to the next are not reported as missing.
