# Gnosis TMS 0.8.105

## Faster alignment when adding a translation

Add translation now splits combined paragraphs in batches of up to 50, subject
to text-size limits. Larger batches can run concurrently, and completed results
are reused when retrying an interrupted alignment.

Alignment preserves your original text. The AI chooses how to split and merge
it to match the existing rows, without correcting capitalization, punctuation
or wording. You can clean up sentence fragments yourself after alignment.
The unnecessary **Edit text** recovery button has been removed.

## More reliable retries

Incomplete or malformed alignment responses are checked before anything is
applied. Failed split batches retry in smaller groups while keeping validated
results. Connection, authentication and rate-limit errors stop the request
without starting extra retries.

Alignment prompts also carry less internal metadata, reducing unnecessary
input tokens. No automatic review flags or review filters are added.
