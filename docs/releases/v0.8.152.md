# Gnosis TMS 0.8.152

## Restoring deleted rows

- You can read a deleted row or its footnote and keep editing the rest of the chapter. Expand **Deleted rows** and click **Restore** to put the row back in its original position.
- **Reviewed** stays available on active rows, and you can open **Mark Unreviewed** while a deleted row is selected.
- **Delete** and **Restore** now respond to the first click when a text field is open. Restoring a row also lets an open edit on a neighboring row finish saving.
