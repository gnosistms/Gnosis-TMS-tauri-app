# Gnosis TMS 0.8.139

## Book export

- The table of contents in step 1 now shows the page as the PDF typesets it, next
  to fields for its title and whether to include H1 headings, like the title page.
- Its page numbers are estimated by typesetting the book's text without images,
  so in books with many images they can run a little low. The exported PDF has
  the exact numbers. Editing the contents title updates the page without
  typesetting the whole book again.
- The title page and table of contents show "Rendering…" with a spinner while
  the page is being typeset.

Includes [#411](https://github.com/gnosistms/Gnosis-TMS-tauri-app/pull/411).
