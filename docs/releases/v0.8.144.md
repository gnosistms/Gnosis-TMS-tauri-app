# Gnosis TMS 0.8.144

## Audio and video

- A row can now hold audio or a video as well as an image. "Insert image,
  audio, or video by URL" takes MP3, M4A and WAV links, YouTube, Vimeo and
  VideoPress videos, and video files, including files in a WordPress media
  library. "Upload image or audio" also takes MP3, M4A and WAV files.
- Audio rows show an audio tile and video rows a poster with a play button, in
  the editor and in the Preview. Clicking one opens a player.
- HTML export plays audio and video in place, and EPUB includes uploaded MP3
  and M4A audio. WordPress export adds audio, video and embed blocks, and
  uploads the project's own audio files to the media library, reusing them when
  you export again. DOCX, RTF, PDF, Markdown, Vellum and plain text show an
  "Audio:" or "Video:" line with the link and the caption, as EPUB does for WAV
  files.

## Editor

- The insert footnote button shows [1] instead of *.
- After you insert a WordPress image link, "Searching WordPress for an image
  footnote." shows with a spinner beside the image while its caption is looked
  up in the media library.
- The Preview tab sets paragraphs as the book does: each one opens with a
  first-line indent, with no blank line between them.

## Book export

- Every body paragraph is now indented, including the first one after a
  heading, quote, citation, list, separator or image. Only the paragraph that
  opens a chapter with a drop cap starts flush.
- Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic and Persian chapters had neither indents
  nor space between paragraphs, so nothing showed where a paragraph began.
  Every paragraph is now indented as books in that language are: two
  characters for Chinese, one for Japanese and Korean, and 1.5em on the right
  for Arabic and Persian.
