# Gnosis TMS 0.8.141

## Editor

- The editing toolbar has two rows: paragraph and text styles on top, with
  "Command + S to save" at the right end, and the inserts (footnote, link,
  images, separator) below. The separator button shows the flourish it
  inserts.
- Cmd/Ctrl+S saves everywhere, including the image URL field and the glossary
  and QA term editors, which used Shift+Return.
- The AI Assistant sends a message on Return. Shift+Return starts a new line.
- Languages you hide in the editor stay hidden for that chapter after a
  restart, and no longer carry over to the next chapter you open.

## Glossary matching

- A standalone … between words of a target variant now allows up to four
  words there, separated only by spaces. For example,
  "sự giằng co … giữa các mặt đối lập" matches
  "sự giằng co dữ dội giữa các mặt đối lập". The source term is no longer
  flagged as untranslated, and the underline covers only the variant's own
  words. The AI translator is told to place modifying words at the …, or to
  join the parts directly when there are none.

## Glossary Research

### Running research

- When the AI recommends running or skipping a step, the panel counts down for
  10 seconds and then takes that advice. Opening the reasoning holds the
  countdown. Run and Skip can be chosen before the advice arrives.
- Steps that only report findings (3, 4.1–4.4.3, 5 and 7) continue on their
  own when their results arrive. Their reports stay in the outline.
- A DLE page the reader cannot read shows a Close button instead of keeping
  the dictionary window open for two minutes.

### Meanings and existing entries

- Step 2b asks what to do about matching glossary entries, names the term in
  each choice, and preselects researching it taking those entries into
  account. That choice no longer loops between steps 2b and 2a.
- "Choose a meaning" has one dropdown of meanings plus "Enter new meaning
  below", and one Assign meaning button. It starts on the AI's suggestion.
- After each meaning you assign, the remaining unmatched occurrences are
  classified again using your answers, so settled uses are not asked about
  again.

### Testing translations (steps 8 and 9)

- Steps 8 and 9 translate with the saved glossary entries that match the
  research paragraphs. An occurrence that a longer saved entry covers, such as
  "antítesis" inside "batallar de las antítesis", is not tested.
- A translation where the target grammar puts a word inside a confirmed form,
  such as an adjective, passes. The form then gets a … at that position.
- Step 8 is skipped when there is one meaning with one candidate word.
- Each review lists the translations that used the correct meaning and those
  that did not, with the reason.
- When repairs cannot make every test pass, "Review the errors yourself" opens
  the findings. Each finding can be marked acceptable or ignored, and in step 9
  the glossary entry can be edited. The other button continues with more AI
  rounds.
- When step 9 reaches its repair limit, it restores the version of the entry
  with the fewest failures.
- When step 9 needs a translation that step 8 never confirmed, it asks you to
  confirm it and retest, or go back to step 8, instead of stopping with an
  error.
- Step 8 no longer slows down while it redraws. Completed step 9 batches fold
  away, and rows no longer keep spinning after a step stops with an error.

### Smaller changes

- The translation summary in translate steps shows only the paragraph count
  until it is opened.
- Each 10a usage count is shown under its translation.
- Step 5 says its verified expressions are used as context for step 6.
- The source-summary skip rule is no longer repeated in steps 4.4.2 and 4.5.
