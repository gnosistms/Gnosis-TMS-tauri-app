# Gnosis TMS 0.8.132

## Quote attribution

- Add a separate attribution for each language in a Quote row. Attributions are
  right-aligned at 90% of the normal text size; quote bodies retain their normal
  size, indentation, and italics.
- Preserve attributions through saving, sync, conflicts, history, row merges,
  preview, and exports, including native Vellum attributions and WordPress
  citations. HTML export retains the attribution after a trailing separator.
- Move the attribution into the body when changing a Quote to another style.
- Filter the editor by Quote block or Heading to find styled rows quickly.

## Editing and subtitles

- Use Command+B/I/U on macOS or Ctrl+B/I/U on Windows to toggle bold, italic, and
  underline in editable text, footnotes, and captions.
- Hide unsupported formatting controls for SRT files and keep formatting
  shortcuts inactive in subtitle and custom HTML editors.
- Preserve intentionally blank subtitle fragments when adding translations and
  exporting SRT files, including their original timing.

## Fixes

- Save image conflict resolutions correctly for linked images, uploaded images,
  and image removal.
- Keep project notices inside their cards without gaps above file rows.
