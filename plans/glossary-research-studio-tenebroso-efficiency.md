# Tenebroso measured efficiency experiment

## Objective
Research Spanish `tenebroso` into English in Test team 32 through the real Studio UI. At each automated step, pause for a quality and cost review; use at most three total executions to compare justified refinements. Preserve earlier studies and original evidence. Use the configured Research provider.

## Procedure
1. Record an unmodified baseline for each step: elapsed/tool/AI/save time, input/output tokens, evidence volume and findings. Distinguish cache reuse from actual recomputation.
2. Inspect local CPU, serialization, prompt duplication and excessive search evidence while each baseline runs. Profile copies of saved data without modifying real studies outside the app.
3. Define the quality criteria before refining each step: retain evidenced senses, source provenance, variant coverage, uncertainties and rare decisive examples. Use diverse bounded evidence with explicit expansion for unresolved questions; keep the agreed final validation sample.
4. Implement focused improvements with meaningful unit/browser checks. Re-execute through an explicit experiment action that preserves baseline results and actually reruns affected tools; do not present cache hits as speedups.
5. Record up to three executions per step, compare quality and costs, select the best justified result, and continue. Keep human/paused time separate. Report regressions, uncertainty and untested claims.
6. Complete the procedure, report measured reductions and remaining bottlenecks, and stop the task's keep-awake process.

## Guardrails
- No unrequested provider/key changes or paid calls unrelated to this study.
- No modification of prior fatal research or historical raw evidence.
- Avoid parallel work that raises local CPU without evidence of benefit.
- Changes to research evidence limits must remain explicit and preserve coverage through adaptive expansion.
