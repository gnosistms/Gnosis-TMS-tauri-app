## Scope and limits

Build in scripts/glossary-research-lab so the experiment can run without changing unrelated dirty application files. This prototype does not retire full frequency counting or two-judge measurement in a production research mode. Conditional replacement methods are experimental. The test suite must reject name-to-definition replacement, missing/incorrect spellings and plurals, loss of rare meanings, false-friend rendering, dropped human terminology, planet over-application and unverified provenance. Source spelling correction and target canonicalization have distinct roles. Every repair is versioned; edited dependencies invalidate judgments.

## Findings carried into final delivery contract

- Source grouping is advisory. The original attested meaning inventory is immutable and remains the coverage authority; source grouping must not erase defining conditions.
- The compiler owns confirmed source identities and verified inflection. An unresolved-plural explanation is not a form; an English original designation is not automatically a Spanish alias. Hasnamuss's verified plural is an actual target choice, not merely report prose.
- A source-only form study may leave a rare borrowed spelling unresolved. After original verification, a bounded identity check can resolve the observed author variants without repeating unrelated dictionaries/frequencies. Exact hits do not end spelling discovery.
- Actual selection failures, not candidate counts, exposed missing matches for Hanasmussen and affective versus sensory amor sensible. Source-only development judgments can supply a newly uncovered sense; confirmation remains withheld from repair.
- The same neighboring source context must reach every translation arm. The first paragraph-only benchmark is retained and separately labelled; it is not the final application-context test.
- Equal context alone did not repair fatal's death case: a blanket construction note for triangles defeated the death-specific rendering. Mandatory human constraints are preserved once in global guidance, with explicit priority over conflicting generic/construction notes.
- Initial and failed attempts, service throttling, unknown usage and protocol-invalid outputs remain in the cost/audit record. No failed, ambiguous or not-run test is converted into a pass. No original full step is retired by the pilot.
