# Improve the full research procedure without losing its safeguards

Hans's 2026-10-13 amendment: begin with the full deep research procedure. Improve quality first by carrying useful findings through proposal, review, counting, consistency, report, saved glossary and actual translator input. Then improve speed and tokens. Do not substitute an untested compact pipeline for that starting point.

1. Freeze the current full engine and report code separately from older experiments. Reuse the source-only 83-term union, explicitly prioritizing historical failures and the human-criterion shortlist (vanidad, Íntimo in VI/EN, fatal, mundos, Hooltampanas). Random terms are a separate regression cohort. Preserve no-glossary controls and zero/sparse/overlapping cases.
2. Before testing any optimization, write a safeguard contract for every full-procedure step: its original error, documented example, observable failure, expected outcome and required evidence. Source words alone are insufficient coverage: include actual rare senses, noun/adjective contrasts, ordinary uses, compounds, lexical contrasts and source-book names. Human criteria define acceptance; inferred criteria stay labeled and available for human correction.
3. Validate the tests by injecting each intended mistake. Known wrong sense, missing minority sense, dropped candidate, lost provenance, opaque unannotated wording, stale selection notes, wrong spelling and unrelated edits must fail. Include acceptable alternative wording, identical good and identical bad controls; randomize/mirror evaluator presentation. An evaluator that accepts its injected fault cannot establish success for that safeguard. Generic negation controls alone do not calibrate subtle terminology tests. AI assessments remain provisional where human meaning/wording judgments are needed.
4. Audit existing full-run checkpoints and replay real engine/report helpers locally. Record every disposition from detected meaning/candidate through the consuming prompt. Retain stable meaning/candidate identities and operational selection constraints. Low counts, representative grouping and terse summarization must not silently erase supported distinctions. Rejected findings need a specific evidenced reason; findings only displayed in the report do not count as reaching the translator.
5. Test one generic preservation repair first, retaining the full stage sequence. Compare its final translations with the same no-glossary drafts, never another research tool as the primary baseline. Measure semantic gains/remaining/new errors, human-required terminology and canonical spelling separately, plus naturalness, completeness, actual tokens and elapsed time. Test all named cases even when the no-glossary translation is already correct; a tie establishes no benefit.
6. Only after the preservation baseline and evaluator controls pass, register efficiency changes one at a time: remove repeated evidence from prompts, reuse exact same word/sense measurements, parallelize independent work, replace model formatting/counting with code, and consider combining/removing calls. A step can be retired only when its specific calibrated acceptance cases pass, preserved findings reach the saved entry and consumer, and regressions remain within the predeclared quality bar. No aggregate random-sample score can override a failed safeguard. Keep a stage when its replacement is unproven.

Scope: experiment code, snapshots, tests and reports only. Current app edits and historical runs remain untouched. No production glossary writes, commits, releases or API billing. Hans explicitly approved sending source excerpts and test criteria through his ChatGPT subscription on 2026-10-14, after the earlier automatic review rejection.

Passage selection: reuse historical baseline drafts; add source-selected challenge passages for coverage gaps, keeping original controls immutable and generating separate neutral drafts for additions. Human-approved criteria are withheld from de novo research but supplied to assessment. Store both initial and repaired outcomes. Missing cases, missing controls, unknown results and disputed non-ties block retirement rather than becoming passes.
