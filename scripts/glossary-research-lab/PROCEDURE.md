# Proposed conditional research procedure

**Required success criterion:** a glossary must improve actual translations over the same passages translated with no glossary. Count confirmed correctness gains and harms against the exact neutral drafts, then net gain; report naturalness separately. Ties earn no credit, and matching another glossary, entry completeness or cost savings alone does not establish success. Earlier method-vs-method findings below describe relative behavior pending the new no-glossary control. The fresh automatic confirmation report, ADAPTIVE_RESULTS.md, leads with that control.

The full random-sample comparison and the separate recent-term comparison are complete. The tested default is one guarded proposal over up to 12 raw passages with no dictionary acquisition or routine audit. See RESULTS.md, RECENT_RESULTS.md and FOCUSED_RESULTS.md for evidence and limits. Attached-pronoun recall, bounded source-book provenance, Spanish dictionary evidence, and a short source-only coverage scout have now been tested on selected terms. Their general routing thresholds remain proposed policies.

1. Find corpus occurrences and confirmed inflections locally. Deduplicate exact text, retain document references, and record the number of distinct passages. With zero hits, try targeted spelling/article/phrase variants or ask for an alias/example before stopping. This fallback is proposed; the current zero-hit case was retained without validation. Keep the zero-hit result visible.
2. Generate a glossary directly from up to 12 raw passages. Use every available passage when there are 12 or fewer. Preserve each supported meaning, semantic category, proper names, and distinct lexical labels in definitions/comparisons. Do not infer expanded initials or bake contextual adjectives into the headword. Return concise targets, context notes, and explicit uncertainties.
3. Evaluate whether extra work is justified. The tested audit gate triggers on competing target variants or a source form shared across meanings; it bypasses a single unambiguous target. A short skeptical audit can back-translate candidates against the same raw evidence. Adopt it only if its measured quality gains justify the extra call.
4. Keep dictionary lookup optional. The random-sample dictionary-removal test supports this default. For unresolved senses, fetch a small published Spanish dictionary section directly, without its translation tables. In the focused cases this costs about a second of local retrieval and no acquisition model call. A web-enabled DLE attempt failed and consumed 113,598 input tokens; the direct fetch is the better tested acquisition path. Dictionary evidence alone did not fix missing coverage for fatal or vanidad.
5. Check the usable research pool before treating one passage as sufficient. In the recent test, reserving 40 evaluations left Regenerar only one research passage. A mechanical search for corpus-confirmed attached-pronoun forms (regenerarse, regenerarnos, and related forms) recovered enough nonreserved evidence for the same 12-passage prompt. Its repaired entry tied the saved app output on all 40 correctness and naturalness comparisons and had five confirmed correctness wins against the original compact entry. This supports a cheap source-recall check for an infinitive with insufficient context, tested on this one term only. Broader expansion for other kinds of unresolved ambiguity remains proposed future work.

The compact methods omit separate word-family research, origins, everyday-language sense classification, extended candidate generation/checking, automatic discovery rounds, repeated candidate-counting passes, repeated translation consistency tests, rare-translation footnotes, and a separate summary. This is a tested replacement of the combined workflow; it does not independently prove every omitted stage useless for every term.

For a term with unresolved meanings, use one brief Spanish-only coverage scout over bounded windows from nonreserved passages, asking it to identify evidenced senses and select at most twelve full contexts. Then run the unchanged proposal prompt on those contexts. Charge both calls. This found fatal's inevitability sense and repaired vanidad's three confirmed rare-sense losses. Simply increasing fatal from twelve to thirty full contexts missed the same sense. Broader rollout requires a validated coverage gate and batching/caps for very large corpora; the tested scout pools here contained only 247 and 114 passages.

For technical noun/adjective ambiguity or a suspected borrowed term, query the independent source-book pair index before commissioning broad origins research. The Íntimo trial used a printed Innermost/Dayspring pair, matching original passages, and at most three books/six original contexts. One new proposal identified the source relationship and distinguished noun/adjective usage. A printed pair alone does not prove first coinage, exclusivity, or a direct borrowing route. Machine-aligned pairs require additional caution. No Gnosis Vietnamese targets enter this lookup.

Do not use context diversity as a universal replacement for coverage. Twelve diverse passages found hành tinh for mundos but dropped rare vanidad senses and narrowed a broad celestial list. A generic scope warning alone left the latter error intact; adding Spanish dictionary evidence with the same guard and contexts fixed the confirmed error. Keep broad and narrow translations with explicit usage notes. The separate exact-plural audit supports a substantial celestial/planetary branch, without establishing a universal planetary default.

Evaluate entry completeness separately from translation preference. A forty-passage tie can miss a rare sense or an origin, and both translations can contain the same material error. Retain absolute error grades and the initial failures, and second-check every claimed preference with a fresh blind judge. The focused repairs are exploratory development on the user's selected cases, not a fresh confirmation sample or proof of universal equivalence.

A term appearing only three times does not need a large sample or repeated counting merely because those stages exist in the old procedure. It needs all three passages and a usable candidate. A rare ambiguous term may still justify one focused check; frequency alone does not establish simplicity. The current study explicitly includes sparse terms instead of hiding them through replacement or repeated sampling.

For future sampling, reserve evaluation by source location or document and suppress overlapping context windows, rather than relying only on full-text hashes. The current frozen experiment retains and reports the exposure audit; changing its sample after judging would undermine the comparison.

Before relying on the old counting stage, validate that each occurrence is assigned to an entry and that unresolved/failed outcomes cannot masquerade as agreement. The V.M. reproduction shows why this precondition matters. A counting repair remains outside this experiment's production scope.

The uncurated truncation pilot is de novo and unseeded. Its losses against the revised human-seeded reference cannot, by themselves, be attributed solely to counting removal: human candidate information also differs. The separate unseeded-parent comparison isolates truncation more closely.

The tested audit gate fired for 24 of 29 positive-hit terms, so it was not very selective. The direct audit-vs-parent development pilot had 142 ties and no clear differences. The full audited method maintained the seeded-reference quality bar but supplied no evidence here that a routine second call is necessary. Keep an audit optional rather than making it the default.
