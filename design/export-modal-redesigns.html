Option 1 · Revised

Quiet rail

A cleaner version of the original two-pane structure. One destination expands at a time, so File, Clipboard, and Transfer always remain visible while the right pane holds the selected format's settings.

Best fit if: you want the least behavioral change and the easiest path from the current implementation.
Prototype action complete

Option 2

Format gallery

Destination tabs narrow the choice, then clear format cards provide recognition and a short explanation. Settings remain visible in a stable inspector on the right.

Best fit if: export options are discoverability-sensitive and users benefit from seeing what each format is for.
Prototype action complete

Option 3

Guided flow

The choice is framed as three calm decisions: destination, format, settings. Everything still happens in one dialog—this is structured guidance, not a multi-screen wizard.

Best fit if: people export infrequently, or the growing set of formats currently feels like too much at once.
Prototype action complete

Option 4

Compact workspace

Top tabs and horizontal format chips use less space and keep the action area close. It feels more like a focused tool panel than a settings dialog.

Best fit if: experienced translators use export often and quick switching matters more than format explanations.
